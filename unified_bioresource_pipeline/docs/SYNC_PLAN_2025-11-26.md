## Background

The unified_bioresource_pipeline was created on Nov 20, 2025 as a consolidation of the bioresource discovery pipeline. However, critical bug fixes and new features were developed in pipeline_synthesis_2025-11-18 between Nov 21-25, 2025, which were never propagated to unified.

### Critical Issue
Script 02b_update_set_c_with_entities.py fixes a data loss bug affecting 1,247 baseline papers (94.9% of all losses). This fix exists only in pipeline_synthesis and is currently UNTRACKED in git.
